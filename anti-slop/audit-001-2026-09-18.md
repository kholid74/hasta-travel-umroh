**#5 R-18 / R-38 Fictional testimonial.**

**#6 R-36 / R-17 Facts asserted about real hotels.**
`content/hotels.ts` pairs real hotel names (Fairmont, Pullman ZamZam, Hilton Suites, ...) with `route` and `stepFree` values, and the UI says "Angka di bawah bisa Anda cek sendiri di peta" (`[slug]/page.tsx:117`). "Bebas tangga" and "menyeberang jalan" cannot be verified from a map, and they are claims about real businesses that matter to wheelchair users.
**Owner decision:** add a source per hotel, or label these fields "perkiraan, belum diverifikasi" in the UI, or use fictional hotel names.

**#7 R-36 / R-38 Invitations to a fictional office.**

Fix: reword to "Alamat kantor (contoh)" and "Rekening (contoh)" in these blocks, or tag the block itself as demo.

**#8 R-35 No recorded verification.**

## MEDIUM

**#9 R-04 Lucide icon set.**
`docs/DECISIONS.md` section 7 picks Lucide for its uniform stroke, with no per-icon relevance written. Per R-37 this is a named pattern, so it needs an owner answer. Used: `ArrowRight`, `ExternalLink`, `ShieldCheck`, `SlidersHorizontal`, `Footprints`, `Check`, `X`.
`Footprints` (walking distance) and `Check`/`X` (included/excluded) are relevant. `ShieldCheck` on the trust block is the generic trust glyph.
**Owner decision:** keep the set with the reasons written into DECISIONS.md, or replace/remove `ShieldCheck`.

**#10 R-08 Decorative arrows.**
`ArrowRight` on the hero CTA (`page.tsx:72`), "Selengkapnya" (`:175`), final CTA (`:220`), and every `PackageCard` button (`PackageCard.tsx:84`). No stated purpose; a "Detail paket" button that only navigates does not need one.

**#11 R-06 Uppercase, wide-tracked labels.**
`page.tsx:56` and `:153` (`uppercase tracking-[0.2em]`), `PackageCard.tsx:46` (`tracking-[0.12em]`), plus `uppercase tracking-wider` on filter labels (`KatalogClient.tsx:88,116,138,160,181`) and `BlokHotel` (`[slug]/page.tsx:47`). Font pairing itself (Instrument Serif + Manrope, Inter rejected in DECISIONS) has a written reason and is fine.

**#12 R-20 / C-3 Homepage fails the swap test.**
The product's differentiator is distance in meters. The hero says "Perjalanan Dimulai dari Sebuah Niat" and "persiapan yang matang dan layanan yang penuh perhatian", which any travel agency could say. "Niat" appears three times on the home page (hero h1, about h2, about paragraph). The meters idea only shows up in the 3-card section, and the drawn motif (`PenandaMeter`) lives on `/tentang`, not home. The about block (`page.tsx:157-168`) adds unsupported self-description ("informasi yang jelas dan apa adanya").
Fix: lead the hero with the meter thesis or put `PenandaMeter` on home; cut the about copy to what the site demonstrably does.

## LOW

**#13 R-05 / R-14 Uniform containers and repeated demo notice.**
Nearly every block is `rounded-card border border-hairline bg-surface` (FAQ items, requirements, trust, CTA, list rows), and inner pages share one skeleton (h1, demo notice, stack of boxes). The demo notice appears in the page header, the trust block, the footer, and the home strip on nearly every page. One sticky/footer notice plus the Kontak and Tentang lines that DECISIONS section 1 asked for would be enough.

**#14 R-15 Generic link/CTA text.**
"Selengkapnya tentang kami" (`page.tsx:174`), "Lihat paket" (twice on home), "Detail paket" (`PackageCard.tsx:83`). More specific: "Lihat 12 paket umroh", "Buka detail dan pilih tanggal".

**#15 C-4 Small text for the stated audience.**
Key facts use `text-sm`/`text-xs`: seat status, price label (`PackageCard.tsx:76`), hotel meta, filter labels, and 11px on the hero credit, for a product aimed at families of 70-year-old pilgrims. Contrast passes; size is the issue. Raise the price label, seat status, and hotel meta to `text-base` or at least 15px.

**#16 Block 3 Dials and Design Read not declared.**
Add a line to `docs/DECISIONS.md`: `Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1` (or your values), and a Design Read sentence.

**#17 C-4 Disclosure affordance and skip link.**
`<summary className="... list-none">` (FAQ, itinerary, Haji Khusus, `page.tsx:195`) removes the marker and nothing replaces it, so nothing signals that the rows expand. There is also no skip-to-content link before the six-link header.
