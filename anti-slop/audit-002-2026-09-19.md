Skill: `antislop-copywriting`. Scope: every visible string in `app/`, `components/`, `content/`, `lib/whatsapp.ts`, after the audit-001 fixes. Read-only: nothing has been changed. Reply with the numbers to fix.

Priority: R-36 / R-17 (Hard Gate) = HIGH, R-16 / vocabulary = MEDIUM, rhythm and hygiene = LOW.

## HIGH

**#1 R-36 Unverifiable frequency and generalization claims (5 places).**
The site is a labeled demo, but these read as statements of fact about real behavior and nothing supports them.
- `app/faq/page.tsx:20` "Pertanyaan yang paling sering kami terima sebelum jamaah mendaftar"
- `app/page.tsx` FAQ heading "Yang paling sering ditanyakan"; `app/paket-umroh/[slug]/page.tsx:203` "Pertanyaan yang paling sering diajukan"
- `app/tentang/page.tsx:31` "Hampir semua travel menulis 'hotel dekat Masjidil Haram'"
- `components/TrustSection.tsx:74` "selisih belasan juta hampir selalu ada yang dikorbankan"

Fix: the FAQ headings become "Pertanyaan sebelum mendaftar" (one wording on all three pages, and it is true of the content). Tentang: "Banyak travel menulis" is still unsourced, so use "Umumnya iklan paket menulis 'hotel dekat Masjidil Haram'" only if you can source it; otherwise "Sebuah paket bisa menulis 'hotel dekat Masjidil Haram'. Masalahnya, ...". TrustSection: "Selisih belasan juta patut ditanyakan: apa yang dikurangi?" (advice, not a claim about how often).

## MEDIUM

**#2 R-16 Empty claims in the Tentang lead.**
`app/tentang/page.tsx:20-21`: "{tagline}. Hasta Travel menemani perjalanan ibadah Anda menuju Baitullah, dengan persiapan yang matang dan layanan yang penuh perhatian." Nothing here is checkable, and it repeats the tagline that is already in the page title.
Fix: replace with what the page then proves: "Hasta Travel menulis jarak hotel dalam meter, tipe rutenya, dan waktu jalan kaki jamaah lansia, supaya Anda memilih paket dengan angka, bukan kesan." (uses only facts already on the site).

**#3 Self-congratulation in TrustSection.**
`components/TrustSection.tsx:38-39`: "Kejujuran soal ini adalah bagian dari yang sedang kami tunjukkan." Announces a virtue instead of showing it; the sentences before it already do the showing.
Fix: cut the sentence.

## LOW

**#4 Negative parallelism and tailing negations (8 places).**
Some carry a real contrast and should stay; some are the formula.
- Keep (load-bearing): Haji intro "bukan tanggal keberangkatan, melainkan antrean" (`haji-khusus/page.tsx:124`), Tentang "bukan kecepatan orang muda", the whole `quotaNote` on the Mujamalah package, and the Home hero "Hotel 180 meter dari masjid, bukan sekadar 'dekat'" (the contrast is the site's thesis, and I wrote that line in the last pass).
- Rewrite:
  - FAQ intro (`faq/page.tsx:20-22`) "dijawab dengan angka dan ketentuan, bukan dengan ajakan menghubungi admin" -> "dijawab dengan angka dan ketentuan."
  - FAQ answer `content/faq.ts:22` "Batas ini bukan formalitas: tiket dan hotel diterbitkan pada rentang itu" -> "Tiket dan hotel diterbitkan pada rentang itu, ..."
  - FAQ answer `content/faq.ts:32` "Rinciannya dijelaskan sebelum Anda membayar, bukan sesudah." -> "Rinciannya dijelaskan sebelum Anda membayar."
  - Tentang `:65` "bukan hanya dipajang sebagai gambar sertifikat" -> "Nomor izin bisa dicek sendiri di SISKOPATUH."
  - Layout meta description "bukan klaim samar" -> cut the clause.
  - Haji `installmentNote`/Haji page "bukan dipatok di halaman ini supaya tidak menyesatkan" -> "tidak dipatok di halaman ini karena kurs berubah."

**#5 Register for the stated audience.**
"Sold Out" (`lib/format.ts:56`, `tentang/page.tsx:62`), "sold out" (`DetailClient.tsx:102`, different casing), and "seat" (cards, detail, jadwal, FAQ) are English in a site for Indonesian families of elderly pilgrims.
Fix: "Penuh" and "kursi", one casing everywhere. Owner decision: "seat" is common travel-industry Indonesian, so keeping it is defensible; "Sold Out" vs "sold out" should be unified either way.

**#6 Repeated itinerary copy.**
`content/itinerary.ts`: `HARI_HARAM` appears up to 3 times in a row (14-day: days 10-12), `HARI_NABAWI` 3 times (14-day: days 3, 5, 6), with identical title and description each time. Reads as template filler.
Fix: collapse to a range ("Hari 10-12") or give each repeat its own line. New facts would be needed for a real rewrite, so the range option is the one I can do without inventing anything.

**#7 Vague quantities in facilities.**
`content/haji.ts:91` "Rombongan kecil dengan pembimbing khusus", `:59` "Tenda Armina kategori upgrade", `:89` "Tenda Armina VIP", `content/packages.ts:26` "Akomodasi hotel sesuai paket". No number, no definition.
Fix needs real data from you (group size, tent spec). Until then, mark "[REAL DATA]" or cut the phrase.

**#8 Dead marketing data.**
`content/packages.ts` `tags` ("Termurah", "Paling banyak dipilih", "Cocok libur sekolah") is never rendered. If it is surfaced later, "Paling banyak dipilih" and "Termurah" would be unsupported claims (R-36).
Fix: delete the field, or leave it and keep it unrendered.
